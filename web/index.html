Features*

One Command
No installers, no configuration, no signup. Just paste a one-liner and share your terminal instantly.
NAT Traversal
Works through firewalls, CG-NAT, and restrictive networks. Only outbound connections are used.
Self-Hosted
All traffic goes through your own server. No third party sees your sessions. Full control and privacy.
Cross-Platform
Native support for Windows (ConPTY), Linux, and macOS. Single binary, zero dependencies.
End-to-End Encryption
All connections use SSH with Ed25519 keys. Sessions are ephemeral with automatic TTL cleanup.
Interactive Terminal
Full PTY support with resize handling. Works with vim, htop, tmux, PowerShell, and any TUI application.

How It Works

1
Share Your Terminal
Run the one-liner on the target machine. swagSSH establishes an outbound SSH connection and receives a short session ID like swag-a8f2d1.
2
Connect From Anywhere
The operator runs swagssh connect swag-a8f2d1. The relay server bridges the two connections instantly.
3
Full Terminal Control
Get an interactive shell with the target user's permissions. Resize your window and it syncs. Session auto-expires after 1 hour.
